Contested Continents

Starting a new campaign will discard your current progress.

A procedurally generated world of ~80 territories. Lead your nation against four AI rivals and neutral garrisons, under fog of war.

Quick start: Reinforce places free armies each round. Attack commits most of a territory's force, but a width cap limits how many can actually fight — small territories and Mountains especially. Move repositions armies (2 free per turn, then a small gold cost). Buy Army and Tech both cost gold. Watch your Army cap in the top bar. From round 10, you'll choose a Capital — losing it ends your game immediately, so guard it well.

How to play

Reinforce — place new armies on your territories from your automatic reinforcement pool (free). Pool size grows with territory count, continent bonuses, and Logistics tech, but is capped — see Army cap below.

Attack — select a territory: up to all but 1 army commits to the fight, capped by a width limit (whichever is smaller: your territory's own size, or the target's terrain — Mountains cap at 5, Forest/Ice at 8, Plains/Desert at 12). Simplified formula:

Power = Armies × Random(0.85–1.15) × Tech/Terrain bonus
Win chance = Attacker Power ÷ (Attacker Power + Defender Power)

Higher power usually wins, but it's a probability, not a guarantee. Win and most of your force advances into the conquered territory; lose and most of it is lost, with survivors retreating home.

Move — relocate armies between two territories you own that are connected by a land border or a naval lane (anywhere you could attack, minus the rare open-sea reach, which stays attack-only). The first few moves each turn are free (2 by default, more with Movement tech); after that it's 5g per move (also reduced by Movement tech, minimum 1g). The amount moved is fixed, not chosen: 1 army always stays behind to hold the source, and roughly 60% of the rest goes with the move (rounded, minimum 1) — the exact number is shown before you confirm. No longer limited by a hard turn cap — move as often as your free moves and gold allow.

Buy Army — spend 10g to add 1 army to your reinforcement pool (respecting your army cap), ready to place wherever you like via Reinforce.

Terrain — every territory is Plains, Forest, Desert, Mountains, or Ice. Forest and Mountains give the defender a strength bonus (shown as a letter badge on the map: F, M, D, or I — Plains carries none). Use the Terrain View button to color the whole map by terrain instead of ownership.

Army cap — your total army count is capped, shown as Army in the top bar. Each territory you hold raises the cap by an amount that depends on its terrain — Plains raises it the most, Forest and Desert a moderate amount, Mountains and Ice the least. Once you're at the cap, further reinforcements and army purchases stop until you gain more territory (or lose armies).

Tech — advance Fortifications, Siege Engineering, Naval Doctrine, Logistics, or Movement one level at a time: 50g for the 1st level, 100g for the 2nd, 150g for the 3rd (max level 3 each). Movement makes moving cheaper AND more generous: each level adds 2 more free moves per turn (2 -> 4 -> 6 -> 8) and cuts the paid cost by 1g per level.

Gold — income is deliberately small (a fraction of your territory count each round), so spending it on armies, tech, or moves is a real trade-off, not something to do every turn without thinking.

Capital — at round 10, every surviving nation designates one territory as its capital (AI picks automatically; you choose by clicking one of your own territories when prompted — pick somewhere defensible). It grants +3g and +1 reinforcement every round. But if it's ever captured, that nation is eliminated instantly — every other territory it held is annexed by the conqueror on the spot (each keeping half its garrison, minimum 1), no matter how much land it still controlled. Your capital is marked with a ★ on the map.

Fog of war — you only see live information for your own territories and their neighbors. Everything else shows either "unknown" or a stale last-known snapshot. God Mode (toggle at campaign start) disables this entirely, for testing.

Hard Mode — toggle at campaign start. Every AI nation gets +15% reinforcement per round on top of the normal amount, for a tougher game.

Auto-Play — hands your turns to an autopilot (reinforce, attack favorable targets, spend gold, end turn) so you can watch a full game play out. Available anytime, not just in God Mode.

Keyboard shortcuts — R Reinforce, B Buy Army, M Move, A Attack, T Terrain View, P Auto-Play, E End Turn. Disabled while typing in a text field or while a modal is open.
